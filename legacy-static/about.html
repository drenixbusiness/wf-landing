Our story

Built from the driver's seat.

Wildfire Express started the way most good carriers do — with people who had spent years behind the wheel and were tired of hearing "the check is in the mail." We built the company we wanted to drive for: a dry van operation where the settlement sheet is readable, the dispatcher picks up the phone, and a load offer comes with the real numbers attached.

Today we run 53′ dry van freight from coast to coast for a network of more than three hundred brokers and direct shippers. Consumer goods, packaged food, paper, retail replenishment and building products — freight that keeps the shelves full and the miles steady.

We stay deliberately small. Every driver has a direct line to dispatch, safety and payroll, and our leadership still answers the phone. Growth is welcome, but never at the price of the people already on the road with us.

Whether you bring your own truck, take one of ours or lease toward ownership, you join the same board, the same standards and the same promise: run hot, stay honest, get home.

Three principles

What we won't bend.

01

Pay is a promise

Every rate, deduction and reimbursement is on paper before you roll. Settlements land every Friday — no exceptions, no surprises.

02

Safety over schedule

No load is worth an hours-of-service violation or a tired driver. If the weather or the clock says stop, we back you — and tell the customer.

03

A person picks up

Dispatch, breakdown and safety support 24 hours a day, seven days a week, from people who know your truck and your home time.
