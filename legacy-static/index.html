Driver voices

What the drivers say.

Questions

Straight answers.

What do I need to apply?
A valid Class A CDL, at least 12 months of verifiable tractor-trailer experience in the last three years, a clean MVR and the ability to pass a DOT drug screen and physical. The 90-second form is enough to start — we collect documents later.
How fast do you get back to me?
A recruiter calls within 15 minutes during business hours, or first thing the next morning. Orientation can usually be scheduled within a week.
How does home time work?
You tell us the schedule — e.g. 3 weeks out / 4 days home, or every other weekend — and dispatch plans loads back toward home around it.
When do I get paid?
Weekly. The week closes Saturday, settlement is prepared Sunday–Thursday, and direct deposit lands every Friday.
Do you force dispatch?
No. You can turn down a load. We'd rather talk it through and find freight that fits you.
